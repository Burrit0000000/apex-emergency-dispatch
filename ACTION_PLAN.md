## 2. Your 3 Simple Steps (Total Effort: ~15 Minutes)

### Step 1: Claim Your AI Phone Number ($10 - $15)
1. Go to [Vapi.ai](https://vapi.ai) (Free $10 credit to start).
2. Click **Create Assistant** -> paste the contents of `vapi_assistant_config.json`.
3. Buy a local phone number in Vapi ($1.15/month). 
   *Now anyone who dials that number speaks to your AI agent.*

### Step 2: Open the Demo Web App
- Double-click `emergency_dispatch_ai/demo_web_app/index.html` to view your live interactive page in any browser.
- (Optional: Drop the folder onto [Vercel.com](https://vercel.com) or [Netlify.com](https://netlify.com) for a 1-click free public URL).

### Step 3: Send the 3-Sentence Emails
- Open `outreach/cold_email_templates.md`.
- Copy **Template 1** or **Template 3**.
- Swap in your new Vapi phone number.
- Send it to 10-20 local contractors.
