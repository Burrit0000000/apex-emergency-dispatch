Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need to change our existing business phone number?

No. You keep your existing business number. You simply set your carrier to conditionally forward after-hours or unanswered calls to your Apex line.

What if our on-call technician is busy or unavailable?

Apex collects the customer details and logs the priority dispatch. Your technician receives the text alert and can either call back immediately or send a quick canned response indicating your next available arrival window.

How fast does setup take?

Once you activate your line, we configure your company parameters and dispatch routing within 24 hours.
